---
name: Aliena visual direction
description: Project-specific visual requirements for the Aliena Money interface.
---

The app should feel like one yellow/cream file folder against a wine-colored background. The folder needs a visible raised top flap, with horizontal navigation tabs physically aligned along its top edge. Use only subtle paper texture and paperclips; avoid unrelated circles, stacked colored folders, and generic assistant-like styling. The exact reference swatches are Imperial Blue `#011E48`, Olive Green `#6C712E`, Powder Blue `#B8D3E9`, Cream `#FFFDD0`, Burgundy `#48020C`, and Smoky Black `#100D08`.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified that the earlier blue background and separate green/yellow folder layers felt confusing and generic.

**How to apply:** Keep wine and cream as the dominant surfaces. Use the other swatches only as restrained accents, and check the full app frame—not just individual cards—when changing styles.
