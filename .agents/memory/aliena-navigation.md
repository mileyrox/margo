---
name: Aliena navigation
description: Product information architecture for Aliena Money.
---

My Plan is the home dashboard and combines the earlier overview metrics/activity with the plan summary. Tax set-aside is a dashboard tile, not a main navigation destination. Receipt and barcode capture actions live in Better Buys. Income, monthly rent, savings goals, and priorities are locked on the home view after setup; updates belong on the account/profile page opened from the top-right avatar. Profile is an account destination, not a tab in the main navigation.

**Why:** On November 3, 2026, the user requested this separation between the dashboard, Better Buys scanning, and account-only editing.

**How to apply:** Keep primary navigation focused on My Plan, Transactions, and Better Buys. When adding editable financial assumptions, put their edit controls in the top-right profile page while showing their results on My Plan.
