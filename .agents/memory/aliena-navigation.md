---
name: Margo navigation
description: Product information architecture for Margo.
---

Home is the dashboard, combining the useful spending overview with the monthly plan. Primary navigation is exactly Home, Spending, Swaps, and Gigs, with a centered + for receipt scan, barcode scan, and manual transaction entry. First-time setup is a full-screen one-question-per-step onboarding flow and should not reappear in Settings after completion. Profile answers, take-home pay, rent, editable category budgets, tax rate, connected banks, data export/deletion, and sign-out belong in Settings opened from the top-right avatar. Tax set-aside and the savings goal are small Home cards, never main tabs. Keep monthly category budgets user-editable, with no priority-category system or percentage guide. Keep tax education general and the estimate rate user-controlled.

**Why:** On November 4, 2026, the user simplified Margo to four primary destinations and moved all profile and planning edits into Settings, while keeping scan and add actions reachable from the center button.

**How to apply:** Keep first-run setup separate from Settings, show real user data on Home, and keep app-wide actions and settings out of the four primary tabs.
